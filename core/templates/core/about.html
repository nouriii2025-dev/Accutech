About Accutech

Our Approach

Comprehensive solutions, start to finish

We firmly believe in offering comprehensive solutions to the industrial project sector in the region. This is made possible by our inherent technical competence, fully backed by our principals, to support our solutions right from the bidding stage through to the commissioning phase.

Why It Matters

Quality is the essential element in the success of any company — and maintaining it consistently is complex and challenging. Our customers can rest assured that the instruments built into their plants around the world will function reliably and exactly, at all times, so they can focus on their own goals undisturbed.

Milestones

Three decades in the region

1996

Founded as pressure & temperature specialists

Abdulla Bin Hamid Trading LLC begins operations in the UAE with a core team of five, focused on pressure and temperature instrumentation.

2000s

Multi-divisional growth
